## A. Lightning Protection Systems (Questions 1–12)

1. **What does a Lightning Protection System (LPS) do for commercial buildings in the UAE?**
   *Target:* `/expertise/lightning`
   > An engineered Lightning Protection System intercepts direct lightning strikes via rooftop air terminals, conducts impulse currents safely through down conductors, disperses energy into low-impedance earth pits, and suppresses transient overvoltages with Surge Protection Devices. Vision Energy International designs complete systems aligned with IEC 62305, BS EN 62305, and NF C 17-102 across Dubai, Abu Dhabi, RAK, and Qatar.

2. **What is the difference between Conventional Faraday Mesh and ESE Lightning Protection?**
   *Target:* `/expertise/lightning`
   > Conventional IEC 62305 systems use passive Franklin rods, roof mesh conductors, and multiple perimeter down conductors. Early Streamer Emission (ESE) terminals compliant with NF C 17-102 actively trigger an earlier upward leader from an elevated mast, offering a larger calculated protective radius with fewer roof penetrations where approved by the project consultant.

3. **What are the four Rolling Sphere radii under IEC / BS EN 62305?**
   *Target:* `/glossary#rolling-sphere-method`
   > Under IEC 62305-3 and BS EN 62305-3, Lightning Protection Levels correspond to specific Rolling Sphere radii and roof mesh sizes: Class I uses a 20 m radius (5×5 m mesh), Class II uses 30 m (10×10 m mesh), Class III uses 45 m (15×15 m mesh), and Class IV uses 60 m (20×20 m mesh).

5. **How does the Protection Angle Method work in lightning protection design?**
   *Target:* `/glossary#protection-angle-method`

It works well for smaller structures, rooftop chillers, tank vents, masts, and telecom towers before transitioning to Rolling Sphere checks.

## B. Earthing, Grounding & Surge Protection (Questions 13–24)

13. **Why is Wenner 4-pin soil resistivity testing required before designing an earthing grid?**
    *Target:* `/uae/dubai/earthing-grounding`
    > Desert sand, reclaimed coastal sabkha fill, and limestone rock across the UAE show widely varying electrical resistivity. Measuring apparent resistivity with a Wenner 4-point survey lets engineers calculate accurate rod depths, grid geometry, and earth enhancement compound quantities before excavation, avoiding expensive post-installation remediation.

14. **How does Earth Enhancement Material lower earth pit resistance in dry UAE soil?**
    *Target:* `/glossary#ground-enhancement-material`
    > Conductive ground enhancement compounds (ER-01) surround earth electrodes inside trenches or boreholes to dramatically reduce electrode-to-soil contact impedance. Engineered to retain conductivity without leaching into groundwater, these compounds help stabilize ohmic earth resistance measurements across arid summer and coastal conditions.

15. **Why are exothermic welded joints preferred over bolted clamps underground?**
    *Target:* `/glossary#exothermic-welding`
    > Exothermic welding (ER-02) uses calibrated graphite moulds and weld powder to fuse copper conductors, earthing tapes, ground rods, and structural steel at the molecular level. Unlike mechanical clamps that corrode or loosen over time, exothermic connections maintain permanent electrical continuity under severe thermal fault currents.

16. **What is the role of an Equipotential Earth Bar with Disconnecting Links?**
    *Target:* `/glossary#equipotential-bonding-earth-bar`
    > Copper or tinned-copper earth bars (ER-03) centralize equipotential bonding across main electrical rooms, lightning down conductors, structural steel, and metallic piping. Removable single or double disconnecting links allow testing engineers to isolate individual earth pits during scheduled fall-of-potential verification without disconnecting facility safety earth paths.

17. **What does Type 1, Type 2, and Type 3 Surge Protection coordination mean?**
    *Target:* `/glossary#surge-protection-device-spd`
    > Cascading Surge Protection Devices divide lightning and switching energy cleanly across facility tiers: Type 1 SPDs protect main incoming LV service panels against high-energy external impulses, Type 2 SPDs protect sub-distribution boards, and Type 3 or data-line SPDs safeguard sensitive BMS controllers, PLCs, laboratory instruments, and server racks.

18. **Why do buildings still need SPDs when rooftop lightning rods are installed?**
    *Target:* `/expertise/earthing`
    > External air terminals and down conductors handle direct structural strikes safely, but electromagnetic induction and utility switching still inject damaging overvoltages onto incoming power, telecom, and photovoltaic cables. Coordinated Type 1, 2, and 3 SPDs (EL-06) referenced to a bonded earth bar protect downstream electronics.
